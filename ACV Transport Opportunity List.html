Surface 1

Checkout

Problem Potential Solution Questions & Dependencies Priority
Too much cognitive load
Recurring and redundant information is shown throughout checkout. Buyers see more than they need to make a decision.
Show only decision-relevant information. Move secondary details to opt-in or on-demand views. Audit for duplicate content across checkout steps.
 Now
Disconnected checkout experience
Checkout doesn't feel integrated with the VDP or MyACV Won state. Total price isn't always visible. Buyer-arranged transport pricing isn't reinforced when ACV Transport is selected.
Design a cohesive flow across VDP → checkout → MyACV Won. Surface transport cost persistently during checkout. Reinforce ACV Transport price when selected vs. buyer-arranged.
 Investigate
Landing cost requires scrolling
Buyers scroll up and down to find the all-in price. Payment option isn't auto-defaulted when only one is available. Error states are hard to identify.
Persistent price summary visible at all times. Auto-default single payment option to reduce friction. Improve error state legibility with clear, specific messaging.
 Investigate
No digital delivery documentation (eBOL)
Pickup and delivery documents aren't digitized. No secure e-signatures. No centralized storage for easy access and verification.
Digitize pickup and delivery docs with secure e-signatures. Centralized cloud storage accessible to all parties for verification.

Transport Hub

Problem Potential Solution Questions & Dependencies Priority
Card information architecture
Cards don't clearly surface the most relevant info per job status. No visual indication when job info has been updated. No urgency signals for time-sensitive items.
Redesign card hierarchy — define what's most relevant at each job status. Add indicators for recently updated info. Introduce urgency signals. Define refresh strategy (auto vs. manual).
 Now
No carrier or location visibility
Buyers can't see which carrier has their vehicle or where it is during transit.
Surface carrier name and contact info once dispatched. Show last-known location or status with timestamp.
 Now
Inconsistent date display vs. MyACV
Date format or type shown in the transport hub differs from MyACV, causing confusion about which date is accurate.
Audit and align date display across the transport hub and MyACV to a consistent format and source of truth.
 Now
Improve quote CTA
The call to action to get a transport quote isn't prominent enough — users miss it.
Increase visual prominence of the quote CTA. Evaluate placement and copy.
 Now
Off Platform: uninformative banner
The Off Platform banner functions more as a CTA than an informative element. Green color may be sending the wrong signal.
Redesign banner to lead with information, not action. Reconsider color to avoid false positive signaling.
 Now
Off Platform: unavailable service types unexplained
When a service type is unavailable, there's no explanation why — just a dead end for the user.
Add contextual messaging explaining why a service type is unavailable and what the user should do instead.
 Now
Generic error messages
"Something went wrong" appears without context — users don't know if it was VIN entry, quote generation, or something else that failed.
Replace generic errors with context-specific messages tied to the action that failed (VIN lookup, quote request, submission, etc.).
 Now
Quote flexibility
Quotes require a VIN. Off-platform creation doesn't support quoting by year/make/model or finalizing multiple vehicles in one order.
Allow YMM (Year/Make/Model) as an alternative to VIN for quoting. Support multiple vehicle finalization in a single off-platform order.
 Investigate
Sellers can't track pickup
Sellers have no visibility into when their vehicle will be picked up after listing. Once it's posted, they're in the dark.
Provide sellers with pickup-focused tracking — scheduled pickup date, carrier assigned, pickup confirmed. Not the full buyer-level tracking view.
 Investigate
Seller access to secret phrase and release documents
Sellers sometimes need access to the secret phrase or bill of sale to authorize pickup, but the path to get it isn't clear.
Define a seller-specific view of pickup authorization info. Surface the secret phrase or release doc clearly in the seller's context without exposing buyer-level detail.
 Investigate
Card density
Cards may be taking up too much vertical space, limiting how many orders a user can scan at once.
Explore a compact card layout. Consider toggling between compact and detailed view based on user preference.
 Investigate
Post-hour delivery uncertainty
Deliveries outside standard business hours create operational friction when the notification system isn't robust enough to cover them reliably.
Improve notification reliability for after-hours delivery events. Define expected delay SLAs for notifications triggered outside business hours.
 Investigate

Dispatcher efficiency
Dispatchers must hunt down carrier information across systems rather than having a summarized view in one place, increasing response time.
Consolidated dispatcher view: carrier profile, active jobs, capabilities, performance tier, and contact info in a single panel.
 Investigate
Real-time vehicle tracking
Customers want to know where their vehicle is, not just what status it's in. Current status-based updates don't satisfy the need.
Customer-facing tracking portal with real-time location updates. Automated milestone notifications (pickup, in-transit, delivery) via SMS and email.

Surface 3

Load Board

Problem Potential Solution Questions & Dependencies Priority
Filters don't match how carriers think
Carriers plan routes by city, not zip code. No price-per-mile (PPM) range filter exists.
Add city-based filter. Add PPM range filter. Improve filter defaults based on usage metrics.
Would a disclosure type filter help carriers find jobs, or would it cause them to avoid inop jobs they'd otherwise take?
What metrics exist on current filter usage?
 Now
Job cards show too little information
Carriers have to open a modal to see basic job details. Vehicle dimensions and special equipment requirements aren't surfaced upfront.
Surface vehicle dimensions, inop status, and special equipment needs directly on the card. Explore adding quick actions to cards to reduce modal dependency.
 Now
Downloading release slips requires opening each job
Carriers have to click into every individual job to download its release slip — highly inefficient for carriers managing multiple jobs at once.
Allow release slip download directly from the Manage Jobs list. Add bulk download for selected jobs.
 Now
New features not being discovered
Carriers aren't aware of new features — there's no in-app announcement mechanism.
Implement in-app feature announcements via Pendo or a similar tool.
 Now
Notification inconsistency across LB and Manage
Notification behavior differs or is missing between the Load Board and Manage Jobs views.
Audit and standardize notification display across both views. Define when notifications appear, persist, and dismiss.
 Now
Carriers miss new jobs — no real-time awareness
Carriers manually refresh to find new jobs. Jobs near booked routes aren't surfaced. Small carriers lose jobs to faster actors before they can act.
Easy"New Jobs Available" floating button using SSE to push updates matching current filters or carrier profile
EasySurface jobs near recently booked pickups and deliveries
Push notifications or in-app only?

HardProfile-based automatic suggestions; recommended jobs surfaced on the staged jobs page when nothing is staged

Carriers have no way to save search criteria and get notified when a matching job appears on the board.

Stage countdown not visible
Carriers can't clearly see how much staging time is left — they may not realize their hold is about to expire.
Visible countdown timer on staged jobs. Alert when time is about to run out.
Technical blockers to surfacing the staging timer in real time?
Is the staging feature likely to remain long-term?

No bulk status updates
Carriers managing large loads can't update multiple job statuses at once — must open each job individually.
EasyCheckbox multi-select on Manage Jobs; bulk status change via dropdown or popout menu

 Investigate
Payment details are unclear
Carriers struggle to find clear information on whether they're getting paid, how much, and when.
Surface clear payment details, expected payout, and payment status per job. Add a payment history view.
 Investigate
No access to past eBOLs
Carriers can't retrieve previously uploaded electronic Bills of Lading.
Add eBOL history accessible from job history or a dedicated documents section.
 Investigate
No positive feedback or recognition
The scorecard only surfaces what carriers "got wrong." No rewards for delivering early or hitting milestones. No sense of progress or status over time.
Add recognition for early deliveries and milestones (e.g., 5k units delivered). Explore gamification patterns from consumer delivery apps.

Large carriers dominate job access
Large carriers claim jobs before smaller carriers can act. Preferred carrier access can inadvertently reduce competition and leave harder jobs for smaller operators.
Revisit job access sequencing. Explore time-windowed tiers that give smaller carriers a fair window before opening to all. Audit the preferred carrier program for crowding-out effects.
 Investigate
Carrier rating not shown on profile
Carrier performance tier or rating isn't visible on their profile — missing context for both carriers and ops.
Display carrier rating and tier status on the profile page. Surface improvement areas alongside the rating to make it actionable.
 Investigate
Banner notification pattern needs improvement
The current notification banner pattern is insufficient or confusing on the Load Board.
Redesign the banner notification component with a clearer urgency hierarchy. Define when banners appear, persist, and dismiss.
 Investigate
No digital signatures at handoff
Larger shippers want formal digital signatures to confirm vehicle handoffs. Timestamps are captured but e-signatures aren't.
Add digital signature capture to the carrier app at pickup and delivery. Tie signatures to the eBOL as a legal record.
Is this a blocker for any current or prospective enterprise shipper relationships?

Compare vehicles
The ability to compare multiple vehicles side by side was available previously but has been removed.
Bring back vehicle comparison with a redesigned experience. Explore whether it belongs on the card level or as a dedicated comparison view.
 Investigate
Map view for jobs and routes

Map view showing available and assigned jobs relative to current routes. Surface jobs near a planned route. Export or share route to navigation apps (Google Maps, Waze).
How does this interact with TMS and route optimization tools carriers already use?

Surface 4

Carrier App

Problem Potential Solution Questions & Dependencies Priority
No photo or video documentation at handoff
No requirement for carriers to capture timestamped photos or videos at pickup and delivery. No condition verification at the point of handoff.
Require timestamped photos at both pickup and delivery as part of the job completion flow — can't mark a status without photo evidence.
 Investigate
Vehicle identity can't be verified in photos
No automated way to confirm the vehicle in a pickup or delivery photo is the correct one. Photos are increasingly easy to fake or substitute.
EasyAI vehicle identification during photo capture; GPS metadata validation to confirm photo was taken at the correct location; use Monk for vehicle matching
 Investigate
Low connectivity handling
Unclear what happens when a carrier is in a low-signal area and photos or status updates fail to upload mid-job.
Offline queue for photos and status updates — sync automatically when connectivity is restored. Clear in-app feedback about pending uploads.
 Investigate
No role-based access control
Sensitive job and carrier data is accessible to all app users regardless of role. No distinction between dispatcher, driver, and owner access levels.
Role-based access control: limit what drivers vs. dispatchers vs. owners can view and edit. Surface only what each role needs to do their job.
 Investigate
No formal driver authentication at handoff
No way to verify driver identity at pickup or delivery points. No mechanism for drivers to access restricted locations securely.
Digital badges or QR codes for drivers to present at restricted locations. Biometric or device-based verification for high-sensitivity handoffs.
Is there demand from large shippers for formal driver auth, or is the release slip process sufficient for now?
 Long-term

Security & Pickup

Problem Potential Solution Questions & Dependencies Priority
Release slip QR limited to current phone
The release slip QR can only be sent to the user's registered mobile number. Entering a landline returns a cryptic "Release slip is not valid" error. The UI gives no guidance on what number to use.
Clarify UI copy to specify that a mobile number is required and why. Support alternate delivery methods (email, secondary number). Replace the error with an actionable, plain-language message.

Admin

Problem Potential Solution Questions & Dependencies Priority
Search by city not supported
Admin search only supports zip codes — same limitation as the Load Board. Ops teams think in cities, not zips.
Add city-based search across Admin. Align with the same change being made to the Load Board filters.
 Now
Approval possible without linking a carrier
Ops can approve a job without associating it with a carrier, creating gaps in the dispatch record with no system warning.
Add a validation or warning when approving a job without a carrier assigned. Gate final approval behind carrier association where appropriate.
 Investigate
Missing role to create carriers
Some users don't have the right role to create carrier accounts in the admin app — this isn't surfaced until they hit a wall mid-task.
Audit and clarify role-based permissions in the admin app. Surface role requirements before users attempt restricted actions, not after.
 Investigate
Hard to find dealer ID when generating a quote
The dealer lookup in the admin quote generation flow is friction-heavy — finding the right dealer ID takes too long.
Improve dealer search to support lookup by name, email, or partial ID — not just the exact dealer ID.
