EXP Track 1
Transport Off-Platform Hub EXP
Shippers – Buyers Only
Awareness Off-Platform Awareness
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Discovers ACV Transport via the marketing website (acvauctions.com/acv-transportation)
  • Learns about transport services through word of mouth or dealer network
Customer need: Shippers are looking for a reliable transport provider
Operations
  • No direct ops involvement at awareness stage
  • Marketing drives inbound traffic to the ACV Transport landing page
Quote & Order Off-Platform Quote Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Logs in to the ACV Platform as a Dealer
  • Enters vehicle details — single vehicle or bundled quote
  • Chooses zip code locations and service type (transport type)
  • Reviews quote summary — pricing, pickup, and delivery details
  • Confirms and places order
Customer need: Shippers are looking to obtain transportation quotes and place transport orders
Operations
  • Order posts to Carrier Loadboard automatically upon placement
  • If not dispatched, paused, or canceled — ops team investigates with dealers
Tracking Vehicle Tracking Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Tracks transport orders on the ACV Off-Platform Transport Page
  • Searches by VIN or Auction ID
  • Views delivery ETA and status
  • Manages transport jobs
  • Uploads gate pass
  • Sorts by order date
Customer need: Shippers want to view and track transport order statuses
Operations
  • Monitors order status on behalf of dealers when needed
  • Investigates stalled or paused orders
Support Customer Support Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Reports vehicle damage to the Operations team via call or email
  • Requests status updates or escalations on stalled orders
Customer need: Shippers need customer support with their transport orders
Operations
  • Handles transport complaints and status updates through dealer calls
  • Resolves damage claims, disputes, and escalations
EXP Track 2
Transport Auctions / In-App Exp
Shippers – Buyers Only
VDP VDP Journey (Service Awareness)
Dealer
Customer
  • Selects dealership on the Vehicle Detail Page
  • Views transportation quote with costs inline
  • Sees available transport promotions
  • If seller has mandated ACV transport — buyer must use it unless vehicle is oversized
  • Transport cost displayed on Proxy, Bidding, Make Offer, and Buy Now flows
Customer need: Shippers want quick transport quotes and promotions while reviewing vehicle listings
Operations
  • No direct ops action at VDP stage
  • System automatically surfaces transport quote and promotions
Checkout Finalized Auctions Checkout Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Selects default ACV transportation service at checkout
  • Selects delivery address and service type
  • Views and compares transportation costs
  • Completes auction checkout
  • If seller-mandated transport — buyer cannot choose their own carrier
  • If quote cannot be auto-calculated (e.g. oversized vehicle) — buyer must call for a quote or arrange transport independently
Customer need: Shippers must select a transport method for their won vehicles
Operations
  • Manually generates post-auction quotes when system cannot auto-calculate
  • Buyers must request these quotes or contact ops directly
Confirmation Won Auctions Vehicle Confirmation
DealerCarrierOps Staff
Customer
  • Receives confirmation email for won vehicle — includes the Pickup Slip
  • Views order and vehicle details
  • Accesses title status tracking
  • If ACV Transport chosen — receives a real pickup slip with QR code (for pickup security)
  • If own transport — receives a "dummy" pickup slip to manage independently
  • Preferred buyers receive confirmation immediately; non-preferred receive it after payment
Customer need: Shippers want to receive a confirmation of their order
Operations
  • Handles transport complaints and status updates through dealer calls
  • Creates transport order manually if needed (e.g. RMC-originated orders)
Tracking Won Auctions Vehicle Tracking
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Explores ACV Transport value propositions on the Hub
  • Searches vehicles by VIN or Auction ID
  • Tracks delivery ETA and status
  • Views order details
  • Accesses offline customer support and feedback
Customer need: Shippers want to view and track transport order statuses
Operations
  • Handles transport complaints and status updates through dealer calls
  • Monitors flagged or stalled jobs
Support Support (Cancellation & Delivered)
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Cancellation: Buyer receives cancellation confirmation by email and views cancelled order details
  • If an Auction ID is present — buyer receives a release slip within the hour
  • Delivered: Buyer receives delivery confirmation by email and views delivered order details
Operations
  • Handles transport complaints and support for both cancellations and deliveries
  • Addresses issues and provides updates through dealer calls

DealerOps Staff

Purchase Order RMC Purchase Order Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Receives a quote from Operations Manager and Transport Dispatcher — includes a 20% additional charge for ACV Transport services
  • Places the Off-Platform Transport order directly through Transport Hub, or

  • RC sends an email to the transport team requesting admin to create the order manually
Customer need: Shippers want to get a transport quote and place a transport order
Operations
  • Operations Manager and Transport Dispatcher handle transport orders for the dealer using the RMC's ACV account
Tracking RMC Vehicle Tracking Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Dealers contact Operations Manager and Dispatcher to track orders
  • ACV account holders can place orders via Off-Platform Transport Quote directly
Customer need: Shippers want to view and track transport order statuses
Operations
  • Operations Manager uses RMC's ACV account to track transport orders on behalf of dealers
Payment RMC Payment Journey
DealerOps Staff
Customer

Customer need: Shippers receive invoices and make payments

  • RMC Front Office adds transport billing to Accounts Receivable
  • RMC pays ACV Transport after receiving payments from dealers
Support RMC Customer Support Journey
DealerOps Staff

EXP Track 4
Transportation Carriers Loadboard EXP
Awareness Carriers Awareness Journey
S,M,L CarrierOps Staff
Customer
  • Discovers ACV Transport via the carrier sign-up website (acvauctions.com/auto-transport-load-board)
  • Receives introduction, welcome, and re-engagement emails
  • Learns through word of mouth within the carrier community
  • Sees activity in the Carrier Vibrancy Slack channel
Customer need: Carriers want to be aware of and encouraged to join the ACV Transport Carrier Network
Operations
  • Carrier Operations Team conducts outreach to recruit new carriers
  • Monitors the Carrier Vibrancy Slack channel for engagement signals
Account & Onboarding Account Management & Onboarding (MCP)
S,M,L CarrierDispatch ManagerOps Staff
Customer
  • After lead form submission, carrier is automatically redirected to acvhaul.com to complete registration
  • Receives an email to finish onboarding
  • Completes the MCP (My Carrier Portal) onboarding process to finalize eligibility
  • Reads and accepts Terms & Conditions
  • Views carrier status — Preferred or First Carriers
  • Receives welcome and onboarding emails, plus a welcome packet
  • Direct dispatch carriers follow a separate offline onboarding process
Customer need: Carriers want to register to the ACV Transport Load Board
Operations
  • Onboarding Specialist monitors new carriers until they book their first few jobs
  • After booking, OS transfers carriers to Account Managers for follow-up
  • Ops team reviews RSIM information to approve or deny applications
  • Onboarding email sent from Salesforce includes personalized welcome and welcome packet
  • Lead created in Salesforce upon MCP form submission
LB Setup Loadboard Account Setup
S,M,L CarrierOps Staff
Customer
  • Completes three online sign-up forms: Carrier Lead Form, Carrier Packet Compliance Form, and Load Board Setup Form
  • Receives success confirmation or denial via email and phone call
  • Sets up username and password
  • Creates carrier profile
  • Completes payment setup offline via ACH form
Customer need: Carriers want to create credentials to access the ACV Transport Load Board
Operations
  • Credential verification — Carrier Operations reviews compliance packets and other info to determine approval
  • Approves or denies carrier sign-up request
  • Carrier Ops Team uses Account Setup in Salesforce for payment and the Transport Admin Tool to log into the Carrier Loadboard
  • CRS (Customer Relationship Specialist) conducts a welcome call with the carrier
Job List Carriers Job List Journey
S,M,L CarrierDispatch ManagerOps Staff
Customer
  • Searches vehicle jobs using saved searches, filters by pickup and delivery location, and sorts results
  • Views vehicle job details — instructions, pickup & delivery locations, bundling options
  • Evaluates pending offers (Instant Book) and views map link
  • Selects jobs to stage — views job category (e.g. INOP), selects multiple jobs
  • Preferred carriers get early access to certain jobs
Customer need: Carriers want to search and book transport jobs
Operations
  • Monitors the job board to ensure listings look correct
  • Manages hold jobs behind the scenes — hold jobs are not yet ready for release
  • Dispatch team sends jobs offline to preferred carriers via direct dispatch
Staged Jobs Carriers Staged Job Journey
S,M,L CarrierDispatch ManagerOps Staff
Customer
  • Views staged job details before committing
  • Edits pickup and delivery dates
  • Instant books or negotiates rates
  • Removes staged jobs if no longer interested
  • Accepts job or continues searching for more
  • Views map link for route planning
Customer need: Carriers want to stage jobs before booking to evaluate and compare options
Operations
  • Assists with price negotiation when carriers counter-offer
  • Approves or denies payout adjustments in the system
Managed Jobs Carriers Managed Job Journey
S,M,L CarrierDispatch ManagerOps Staff
Customer
  • Views managed job statuses — Active, Pending, Dispatch, Picked Up, Delivered
  • Adds job notes and dealer instructions
  • Updates job status, uploads eBOL, downloads dispatch sheet & release slip
  • Marks jobs as picked up, translates language if needed
  • Switches between table & card view, sees past due statuses, views map link
  • Cancels jobs with reasons and exports CSV
Customer need: Carriers want to manage the jobs they've booked
Operations
  • Helps carriers download release slips and dispatch sheets, and sends documents to them
  • Manages hold and canceled jobs — hold jobs not ready for release
  • Manages carriers and payouts for dry runs — a dry run is a failed or incomplete pickup/delivery attempt where no transport occurs
Performance Carriers Performance Tracking
S,M,L CarrierOps Staff
Customer
  • Receives a monthly scorecard sent via email
  • Receives carrier status updates via call or email — First Carriers, Preferred Carriers, or Blocked Carriers
Customer need: Carriers want to review and track their job performance
Operations
  • High-performing carriers are contacted by Transport for direct dispatch consideration
  • Coaching conversations are held with carriers who have performance issues
Profile & Settings Accounts Settings & Profile
S,M,L CarrierDispatch ManagerOps Staff
Customer
  • Views carrier profile — tier display, carrier & company information, company indicators
  • Manages users — active or pending status, edits user info & role, adds a user
  • Manages fleets — vehicle information and specialty equipment
  • Views account rep information, legal terms, and customer support & feedback
  • Logs out
Customer need: Carriers want to set up their profile and adjust account settings
Operations
  • CRS (Customer Relationship Specialist) and Account Managers handle any carrier issues or concerns offline

Pick Up Carriers Pick Up Journey
S,M,L CarrierDealer
Customer
  • Uses scanner or manual input to enter VIN
  • Scans VIN on the vehicle to display vehicle information
  • Captures required vehicle photos — can add extra photos for additional detail, option to skip required photos
  • Processes the pickup job and marks pickup as complete
  • Dealer tracking status updates automatically to "Picked Up"
Customer need: Carriers need to efficiently pick up vehicles and document condition at origin
Operations
  • No direct ops involvement at pickup — process is carrier-managed via the app
Drop Off Carriers Drop Off Journey
S,M,L CarrierDealerOps Staff
Customer
  • Views active jobs and inputs or scans VIN at destination
  • Captures required vehicle photos — can add extras, adds photo of key location, option to skip required photos
  • Completes drop off and generates eBOL (electronic Bill of Lading — proof of delivery)
  • Views and shares eBOL (PDF) with customer upon request
  • If not using the carrier app — emails eBOL to ACV manually
  • Carriers must submit eBOL within 30 days to receive payment from ACV
What is a BOL? A Bill of Lading is a legal document that serves as a receipt (vehicle was picked up), a contract (transport terms), and a document of title (ownership/responsibility during transport)
Operations
  • Ops team receives the eBOL automatically after drop-off is completed
  • If vehicle is off-platform — carriers must manually upload the eBOL
  • Ops team follows up with carriers who haven't uploaded the eBOL
