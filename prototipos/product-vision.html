01 — The Problem Today

Three stakeholders. Three broken experiences.

Overall Experience Problem

How might we eliminate the friction in our transportation process to create a fast, secure, and effortless experience for dealers, carriers and the Transport operations team?

Business
Growth is being constrained from multiple directions at once.
  • Declining auction attachment: Softening sell-through rates in the core marketplace directly suppress transport attachment rates — fewer auctions means fewer transport jobs.
  • Aged job backlog: Low carrier interest in specific regions drives up aged job volume, creating a tension between dispatch speed and carrier quality. Aged jobs make the service feel slow and unreliable — and block expansion into new offerings like expedited transport.
  • Low bundle adoption: Bundles are a primary lever against aged jobs, but adoption remains low. An underutilized lever leaves the backlog problem unaddressed.
  • Carrier vibrancy gap: A lack of active carriers in key markets leads to less competition, higher shipping prices, and inconsistent service quality — making the marketplace less dependable for everyone.
  • User experience as a retention risk: A poor experience pushes dealers toward competing platforms and carriers toward Central Dispatch or Super Dispatch. Falling behind on features makes the service look outdated before the underlying logistics problem is even addressed.
  • Fraud exposure: Without robust fraud prevention, the platform faces direct financial losses and risks eroding the trust users have in the security of their transactions.
  • Fuel cost volatility: Rising and unpredictable fuel prices pressure carrier margins, making it harder to anchor transport pricing and increasing the risk of last-minute cancellations or route rejections.
  • A marketplace that doesn't match: The current response to aged jobs — raising prices and bundling — treats the symptom. The root cause is a platform that doesn't connect the right carrier to the right job at the right moment.
Buyer
They won the auction. Then the uncertainty begins.
  • No real-time tracking — data lags significantly behind reality.
  • A carrier can deliver a vehicle without the buyer being informed in time.
  • When buyers arrange their own carrier, ACV displays a process it can't actually see.
  • The vehicle security process creates enough friction that users avoid it — a gap ACV is actively working to close with dedicated tooling.
  • Preventing fraud requires a tighter security cycle — one that doesn't rely on falsifiable documentation or unsecured handoff points.
Seller
The vehicle leaves the lot. Then, silence.
  • The Transport Hub offers dealers no information once the vehicle leaves their lot.
  • No pickup confirmation.
  • No delivery update.
  • No visibility at any point after handoff.
Carrier
Professional work. Consumer-grade tools.
  • Tools don't meet the demands of day-to-day professional work.
  • Documents needed in the field — BOL, inspection reports, job paperwork — aren't reliably accessible.
  • The job-finding experience doesn't account for a carrier's lanes, fleet, or track record.
  • The platform treats every carrier the same, regardless of how they actually operate.
02 — The Opportunity

A wide-open market for trust and transparency.

ACV's structural advantage
ACV is the only platform that owns the transaction from auction to delivery.

Most transport platforms are logistics tools disconnected from the sale — they depend on the carrier for data. ACV already has the buyer, the seller, the vehicle, and the timeline in one place. That's a position no standalone logistics platform can replicate.

Business Opportunities
  • Re-couple transport attachment to auction health. Improving the transport experience — pricing clarity, speed, reliability — can recover attachment rates even as sell-through softens.
  • Smart matching as the lever against aged jobs. Connecting the right carrier to the right job based on lanes, fleet type, and track record reduces aged job volume at the root, before price increases or bundling become necessary.
  • Surface bundles earlier in the job-finding flow. Increasing bundle adoption is one of the most direct levers available today — but it requires carriers to discover and understand bundles before they've already committed to something else.
  • UX and feature parity as an active differentiator. A better experience is not just retention — it's acquisition. Carriers and dealers choose platforms. Meeting the bar competitors have set is the floor; exceeding it is the opportunity.
  • Fraud and trust infrastructure as a moat. ACV Verify and digital eBOL are capabilities standalone logistics platforms cannot replicate without the underlying transaction data. Building them well turns a compliance requirement into a competitive advantage.

03 — North Star

What ACV Transport becomes.

A platform where every vehicle move is tracked and accounted for, every carrier is matched to the right job, and every stakeholder has the information they need — without having to ask for it.

Visibility by default
Every stakeholder informed at every relevant moment.

Buyers know where their vehicle is and when it will arrive. Sellers receive confirmation at pickup and delivery. Ops teams are alerted before problems escalate. Status updates are automatic, accurate, and timely — not dependent on someone making a call.

Smart matching
The right job, surfaced to the right carrier — before they have to search.

The platform learns each carrier's lanes, fleet type, and pricing patterns, and proactively suggests jobs that fit. As the network grows, matching improves. Jobs move faster, aged inventory declines, and carriers build a more predictable, profitable business on ACV.

Professional infrastructure
Tools built for the scale and complexity of professional transport work.

From the owner-operator booking a single load to the fleet dispatcher managing fifteen drivers — the platform meets each one at their level of complexity. Documents always accessible. Payments reliable and fast. Processes that don't require workarounds.

04 — Future Experience

The Buyer: from auction win to driveway — no calls needed.

Transport is already triggered the moment the auction closes. The North Star isn't about changing that flow — it's about making what happens next visible in real time.

In transit
  • Pickup confirmed in the platform the moment it happens.
  • ETA updated in real time as conditions change.
  • Delays surfaced proactively — before they affect the buyer's planning.
Delivery
  • Delivery confirmed the moment it happens — no more "in transit" on a vehicle already on the lot.
  • The eBOL is generated in the carrier app and uploaded manually — the North Star is documentation available to the buyer the moment delivery is confirmed, without depending on a manual step from the carrier.
Off-platform opportunity

There is an opportunity to explore how ACV can better support buyers who need to place transport orders outside the auction flow — both for single vehicles and at scale.

05 — Future Experience

The Seller: a clean handoff — and confirmation it was received.

Sellers are not active users of the platform after the sale. The North Star for them is simple: be informed at the two moments that matter.

Pickup
  • Seller is notified the moment the carrier picks up the vehicle from their lot.
  • No more uncertainty about whether the handoff happened or when.
Delivery
  • Seller receives confirmation when the vehicle is delivered to the buyer.
  • The transaction is closed — no open questions, no follow-up needed.
06 — Future Experience

The Carrier: professional tools for the backbone of the network.

Carriers are the operational backbone of transport — yet the platform today doesn't reflect that. The North Star is a platform that works at every level of the carrier business.

Job matching
  • The platform learns each carrier's lanes, fleet type, and pricing patterns.
  • Jobs are surfaced proactively — the right load, at the right moment, without manual searching.
  • As the network grows, match quality improves for every carrier on it.
Documents and field operations
  • BOL and job paperwork always accessible — before, during, and after the job.
  • Pickup instructions accurate and complete: address, gate codes, vehicle location, contact.
  • Issues resolvable in the app without making a call to ops.
Tools at scale
  • A desktop-first dispatcher view: see all available jobs, assign loads to drivers, track fleet status in real time.
  • The mobile app fast and reliable enough to book a load in one tap — critical for owner-operators who can't afford to miss a job.
  • Real-time tracking and status updates available from the mobile app — carriers can report pickup, delivery, and delays without friction.
  • A driver-specific flow optimized for field work: step-by-step instructions, eBOL completion, and status updates in a single uninterrupted sequence.
Open question

Should ACV provide carriers with financial visibility into their earnings and profitability on the platform? Fleet owners consistently ask for it — the question is whether this belongs inside ACV Transport or in a separate business tool.

07 — Guiding Principles

The beliefs that shape every decision.

01
Design for the moment, not the user

Context changes what people need. We design for what someone is doing right now, not who they are.

02
One surface, one job

Every screen, card, and modal has one job. When a surface tries to do more than one, it ends up doing none well.

03
The information we show is a promise

Every piece of information we show carries a level of commitment. We know which data must be exact, and which is directional — and we design accordingly.

04
The interface anticipates, don't wait

When users arrive with a clear goal, we surface what they need before they have to look for it.

08 — Strategic Bets

What we need to build to get there.

Six capabilities that unlock the North Star — each one a prerequisite for the next.

Bet 01
Real-time tracking infrastructure

The foundational layer. Without accurate, timely location and status data, every other visibility promise falls apart. This means pickup and delivery confirmations that happen the moment they occur — not hours later — and an ETA that reflects reality, not a static estimate set at dispatch.

Bet 02
Smart job matching

Job suggestions already exist in a basic form — surfaced via email, a separate page outside the loadboard, and as early access for preferred carriers. The bet is to expand and deepen this system: bring suggestions into the core loadboard experience, extend them to all carriers while preserving preference tiers, and move from static suggestions to real behavioral matching based on lanes, fleet type, and booking patterns.

To investigate

How does the Transport Intelligence Engine actually work today? What signals drive suggestions, how are preferred carriers selected for early access, and what is the current reach and engagement of the email and page surfaces? Needed before scoping the expansion.

Bet 03
Driver mobile flow

A streamlined, field-first experience for company drivers and owner-operators: accurate pickup instructions, eBOL completion, and status updates in a single uninterrupted sequence. Fast enough to use from the cab. Reliable enough that drivers stop calling ops to resolve issues on site.
