================================================================================
LB COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS: CENTRAL DISPATCH vs SUPER DISPATCH
For: ACV Transport Load Board
Date: March 27, 2026
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Central Dispatch: Aging monopoly load board. Dominant market share in auto
transport. Carriers use it by necessity despite poor UX. Phone-based
negotiation model.

Super Dispatch: Modern all-in-one platform for small carriers. Combines load
board + TMS + BOL + payment. Better mobile and field workflows. Smaller load
volume than CD but growing.


================================================================================
1. LOAD BOARD / JOB SEARCH FEATURES
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Search by origin/destination with radius adjustments
  - Corridor/route-based search available
  - Filters: vehicle type, transport type (open/enclosed), runs/drives status,
    date range, payment type, broker rating
  - Map view + List view toggle (confirmed in screenshots)
  - Saved searches + email alerts (though alerts reported as unreliable)
  - Load listing shows:
    * Posted recency ("Posted X hours ago")
    * Price + $/mile calculation (confirmed)
    * Vehicle year/make/model (confirmed)
    * Weight/dimensions (confirmed)
    * Origin & destination city/state (confirmed)
    * Broker name/rating (not visible on card, must expand)
    * Inoperable/runs-drives status icon (confirmed)
  - Pagination with numbered page controls (confirmed)
  - "USE OLD SEARCH" fallback button available (confirmed)

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Load board search by origin/destination
  - Filter by shipper rating, verified shippers only
  - Filter by vehicle type, transport type, price range, date
  - Corridor/route-based search
  - Map view showing load pins
  - Central Dispatch integration: can browse CD loads within SD platform
  - Real-time load notifications (push-based, faster than CD email alerts)
  - Load details include shipper/broker name, vehicle type, price, date


WINNER: Central Dispatch for load volume + discovery; Super Dispatch for
         UX + real-time alerts


================================================================================
2. JOB BOOKING / CONTRACTING
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Contact-to-book model (NOT instant)
  - Carrier identifies load, calls/messages broker directly
  - Broker and carrier negotiate rate and terms (rate posted is often below
    final bid)
  - Broker sends formal dispatch order once agreed
  - Carrier digitally signs contract in platform
  - Load moves to carrier's active queue
  - Pain point: desirable loads can be taken by another carrier while
    negotiating

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Instant book available on some loads (no negotiation required)
  - Request/counter-offer for others
  - Digital contract generation
  - Super Carrier Score (carrier's reputation) determines which loads accessible
  - Once accepted, load populates dispatcher immediately


WINNER: Super Dispatch (faster, less friction)


================================================================================
3. JOB MANAGEMENT / TRACKING
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Active orders dashboard (pending, in-transit, delivered)
  - Order shows full details: vehicle, addresses, shipper contact, rate
  - BOL/condition reporting available but basic
  - Photo documentation is third-party (not native to CD)
  - Order history searchable, feeds into carrier rating
  - NO real-time GPS tracking built-in
  - Carriers must use separate telematics/ELD for live tracking

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Route planning view showing multi-load trips (confirmed in screenshot)
  - Route table with: Load, Origin, Destination, Vehicles, Pickup Date
    (confirmed)
  - Map view with all pickup/delivery pins (confirmed)
  - Route editable + can assign drivers/vehicles (confirmed)
  - Attachments per trip (drag & drop, up to 20MB) (confirmed)
  - Internal notes per trip (confirmed)
  - Digital Bill of Lading (BOL) with:
    * Photo capture (pre/post vehicle condition)
    * E-signature from customer
    * Cloud storage, accessible anytime
  - Vehicle inspection integration (timestamped photos)
  - GPS tracking for live visibility
  - Payment terms visible per load (confirmed in screenshot)


WINNER: Super Dispatch (BOL + GPS + photo integration native; CD requires
         third-party tools)


================================================================================
4. MOBILE APP
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Mobile app exists (iOS/Android) but widely considered weak
  - Basic load search and filtering on mobile
  - View active orders and dispatch details
  - Contact brokers via phone
  - Push notifications (unreliable)
  - Dated UI/UX; rated 2-3 stars on app stores
  - Heavy reliance on desktop for full workflow
  - No in-app BOL or condition reporting (major gap)
  - Many carriers prefer desktop even in field

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Dedicated Carrier TMS mobile app
  - Full load board browsing + job booking on mobile
  - Digital BOL creation + e-signature capture
  - Vehicle inspection with photo upload (timestamped)
  - GPS check-in at pickup/delivery
  - Push notifications (reliable)
  - In-app messaging with shippers/brokers
  - Payment status visibility
  - Offline mode for BOL/inspection (syncs when connected)
  - Widely praised as best-in-class for field workflows


WINNER: Super Dispatch (modern, purpose-built for field work)


================================================================================
5. COMMUNICATION FEATURES
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - In-platform messaging system (tied to order/dispatch)
  - Phone-first culture: most negotiations happen by phone
  - Broker phone number + company name visible on listings
  - Email and unreliable push notifications
  - No video calling, no automated status updates to end customers
  - Real-time notification reliability is major complaint

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - In-app messaging per order
  - Message threads tied to specific loads
  - Push notifications (reliable)
  - Phone number visibility once load booked
  - Automated status updates to shipper (e.g., "picked up")
  - No native video calling


WINNER: Super Dispatch (more reliable notifications + status automation)


================================================================================
6. PAYMENT / FINANCIAL
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - CD does NOT process payments (critical point)
  - Payment is broker-to-carrier, outside platform
  - Payment terms visible on listings (COD, check, Comchek, ACH, etc.)
  - NO escrow or payment protection through platform
  - If broker doesn't pay, carrier must pursue independently
  - Broker ratings serve as indirect protection (avoid low-rated brokers)
  - Industry culture accepts late/non-payment risk as normal

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Super Pay: escrow-backed payment option
  - Faster payment: 1-2 business days after delivery confirmation
  - No factoring fees (vs. 3-5% from traditional factoring companies)
  - Payments via ACH direct deposit
  - Payment status visible in-app in real time
  - Auto-generated invoices upon delivery
  - Invoice history + export for accounting
  - Works only with Super Pay-enrolled shippers/brokers
  - For non-Super Pay loads, standard payment terms apply (net 30, etc.)
  - Subscription: ~$99-149/month for full TMS access (includes Super Pay)


WINNER: Super Dispatch (eliminates cash flow pain + payment protection)


================================================================================
7. REVIEWS / RATINGS SYSTEM
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Broker ratings visible on load listings (confirmed in research)
  - Numerical scale (out of 5 stars) with written reviews
  - Rated on: communication, payment speed, accuracy, professionalism
  - Carrier ratings also visible (brokers rate carriers post-job)
  - Ratings are primary trust mechanism (no payment guarantee)
  - Retaliation reviews are a concern
  - Manipulation possible by large brokers with volume leverage

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Super Carrier Score: reputation score for carriers
  - Based on: on-time performance, delivery accuracy, BOL completion rate,
    customer reviews, dispute history
  - Works like a credit score; higher scores unlock better load access
  - USDOT/MC verification required (verified badge on profile)
  - Insurance verification (carriers must upload current certificates)
  - Carriers can also rate shippers/brokers post-job
  - Creates vetted marketplace


WINNER: Super Dispatch (more comprehensive, less susceptible to manipulation)


================================================================================
8. PRICING MODEL
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Carrier subscription: ~$95-100/month
  - No per-load commission
  - Annual discount available
  - 30-day free trial for new carriers
  - Perceived as necessary cost (monopoly pricing) despite dislike

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Free tier: basic load board + limited BOL
  - Paid tier (Super Carrier TMS): ~$99-149/month per carrier
  - Includes: unlimited BOLs, Super Pay access, GPS tracking, reporting,
    priority support
  - Some plans are per-user/per-driver, others company-wide
  - No per-load commission
  - Subscription cheaper than factoring fees on net-30 payments


WINNER: Roughly equivalent; Super Dispatch offers more value per dollar for
         small carriers


================================================================================
9. UX STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH STRENGTHS:
  + Dominant load volume (essential for carrier operations)
  + Industry standard (everyone knows how to use it)
  + Broker transparency (ratings, history, contact visible)
  + Route/radius search practical for route planning
  + Long-term order history builds verifiable reputation
  + Pagination + search refinement UI works

CENTRAL DISPATCH WEAKNESSES:
  - Outdated, clunky interface (confirmed visually)
  - Poor mobile experience (relegated to 2-3 stars on app stores)
  - NO instant booking (phone-tag wastes time)
  - No payment processing (zero protection from non-payment)
  - No integrated GPS tracking
  - No integrated BOL/photo tool
  - Unreliable notifications
  - Slow customer support
  - Subscription auto-renewal complaints


SUPER DISPATCH STRENGTHS:
  + Modern, purpose-built interface
  + Mobile app is clean and field-capable
  + Digital BOL workflow (end-to-end, eliminates paper)
  + Super Pay solves cash flow problem
  + GPS tracking native
  + Vehicle inspection integration (photos + timestamps)
  + All-in-one for small carriers (load board + TMS + payment)
  + Offline mode for field work
  + Faster notifications + real-time alerts
  + E-signature from customers (stronger legally than paper)
  + Multi-load route planning (confirmed in screenshot)
  + Attachments/document management (confirmed)

SUPER DISPATCH WEAKNESSES:
  - Central Dispatch still dominates load volume
  - Subscription cost pain point for very small operators
  - Customer support mixed reviews (response times slow)
  - Messaging/communication tools basic compared to dedicated platforms
  - Mobile app bugs reported (crashes on older devices, sync issues)
  - Limited customization (rigid TMS)
  - Steep learning curve for non-tech-savvy carriers
  - Super Carrier Score system felt opaque by some users


================================================================================
10. STANDOUT / UNIQUE FEATURES
================================================================================

CENTRAL DISPATCH:
  - Network dominance (largest load pool in US auto transport)
  - Built-in broker vetting ecosystem (years of reviews = institutional knowledge)
  - Route corridor intelligence (backhaul planning aided by load density)
  - Industry-specific data (vehicle condition, enclosed vs. open classification)
  - Integration ecosystem (Super Dispatch, Revinova, factoring companies)

SUPER DISPATCH:
  - Digital BOL (end-to-end: creation, inspection photos, e-signature, cloud storage)
  - Super Pay (escrow-backed, 1-2 day payout, eliminates factoring)
  - Super Carrier Score (trust system with reputation score)
  - Vehicle inspection integration (photo-based pre/post condition)
  - Central Dispatch integration (access CD loads from within SD)
  - All-in-one TMS for small carriers (load board + dispatch + payment)
  - Mobile-first design (purpose-built for field work)
  - Offline mode for BOL/inspection

================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS FOR ACV TRANSPORT LOAD BOARD
================================================================================

1. MARKET GAP ADDRESSED BY SUPER DISPATCH:
   Central Dispatch controls load volume but has poor UX, no payment processing,
   and terrible mobile. Super Dispatch built the modern alternative but hasn't
   achieved CD's load volume yet.

2. SUPER DISPATCH'S MOAT:
   Digital BOL + Super Pay solve the two biggest operational headaches in auto
   transport (paperwork + cash flow). This is strong enough to keep carriers
   even with smaller load volume.

3. WHAT CENTRAL DISPATCH KEEPS:
   Network effects. Until another platform matches CD's load volume, carriers
   will maintain subscriptions despite hating the UX.

4. OPPORTUNITIES FOR ACV:
   - Seamless integration with existing load boards (don't force choice)
   - Mobile-first design for field carriers
   - Digital BOL/inspection workflow (SD has proven this works)
   - Faster payment options (Super Pay model is validated)
   - Better real-time notifications than CD
   - Modern, clean interface (CD is visually outdated)
   - Emphasis on carrier reputation/score (matches SD model)
   - Multi-load route planning (confirmed useful in SD)

5. RISK:
   Without a partner/integration deal for load volume, ACV would need to
   compete on product alone. SD has great product but still relies on CD
   integration for load availability. CD's monopoly is very strong.

From job management screenshot (Order #96983557):

WHAT ACV HAS (Strengths):
  + eBOL capability (Add eBOL when Delivered)
  + Vehicle photo included with listing
  + VIN display
  + Vehicle price displayed ($259)
  + Downloadable documents (ACV Release Slip, Dispatch Sheet)
  + Status update buttons (Mark As Picked Up)
  + ETA management (Update ETA button, marked "New")
  + Alert system for overdue deliveries (yellow banner with phone)
  + Multi-language instructions support (English dropdown)
  + Clean, readable layout for job details
  + Instructions section with copy-to-clipboard capability


WHAT ACV IS MISSING vs COMPETITORS:
================================================================================

1. EBOL WORKFLOW IS INCOMPLETE:
   - Only "Add eBOL when Delivered" (post-delivery only)
   - Super Dispatch: Photo capture + inspection at PICKUP & DELIVERY
   - Super Dispatch: E-signature collection from customer in-app
   - ACV: No pre-delivery inspection workflow visible
   - Impact: Can't document vehicle condition at pickup (leaves ACV vulnerable to
     damage claims post-delivery)

2. NO GPS TRACKING / CHECK-IN:
   - Super Dispatch: GPS check-in at pickup/delivery
   - Super Dispatch: Real-time location tracking
   - ACV: No visible tracking capability
   - Impact: No visibility into carrier location/progress for shippers

3. NO MULTI-LOAD ROUTE PLANNING:
   - Super Dispatch: Route planning view with multiple loads per trip, map,
     driver assignment
   - Central Dispatch: Basic multi-load support through notes/system
   - ACV: Shows individual job details only
   - Impact: No optimization for carriers managing multiple loads in sequence

4. NO PAYMENT INFORMATION/TRACKING:
   - Super Dispatch: Payment terms + status visible per load, Super Pay option
   - Central Dispatch: Payment terms visible on listings (though no processing)
   - ACV: No payment info visible in job detail screen
   - Impact: Carriers don't know payment terms until completing job

5. NO REAL-TIME PUSH NOTIFICATIONS:
   - Super Dispatch: Push notifications for loads, messages, payment updates
   - Central Dispatch: Email alerts (unreliable)
   - ACV: Only alert shown is overdue warning
   - Impact: Carriers may miss new load opportunities, messages, status updates

6. NO IN-APP MESSAGING WITH SHIPPERS:
   - Super Dispatch: In-app messaging per order with shippers
   - Central Dispatch: In-platform messaging system
   - ACV: Not visible; likely relying on phone/external channels
   - Impact: Communication fragmentation; shippers can't reach carrier in-app

7. NO CARRIER REPUTATION SYSTEM:
   - Super Dispatch: Super Carrier Score (on-time, accuracy, BOL completion, reviews)
   - Central Dispatch: Star ratings from shippers
   - ACV: No visible reputation/rating system
   - Impact: No trust signal for new shippers; no incentive for quality

8. NO EXTERNAL LOAD BOARD INTEGRATION:
   - Super Dispatch: Integrates Central Dispatch loads directly
   - ACV: Appears standalone
   - Impact: Carriers must use separate platform for broader load discovery

9. LIMITED DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT:
   - Super Dispatch: Cloud-backed BOL with e-signatures, photo attachments,
     timestamped records
   - ACV: Downloadable files (Release Slip, Dispatch Sheet) — paper-adjacent workflow
   - Impact: Documents not legally robust; harder to dispute damage claims

10. NO ATTACHMENT CAPABILITY PER JOB:
    - Super Dispatch: Drag & drop attachments up to 20MB per trip
    - ACV: No visible attachment upload in job detail
    - Impact: Carriers can't attach photos, receipts, or other supporting docs

11. NO INTERNAL NOTES PER JOB:
    - Super Dispatch: Internal notes field per trip (visible in screenshot)
    - ACV: Not visible
    - Impact: Can't record carrier-specific context/issues per job

12. UNCLEAR JOB STATE WORKFLOW:
    - "Staged (2)" tab is ambiguous
    - Super Dispatch: Clear states (Assigned → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered)
    - Central Dispatch: Clear dispatch workflow
    - ACV: "Staged" meaning unclear to new users

13. NO MOBILE OPTIMIZATION VISIBLE:
    - Super Dispatch: Mobile app specifically designed for field (BOL, GPS,
      signatures, offline mode)
    - ACV: Appears desktop-first
    - Impact: Carriers can't complete job workflows efficiently in field

14. NO LOAD SEARCH/FILTERING:
    - Screenshot shows job detail, not load board discovery
    - Can't tell if ACV has search filters like CD/SD
    - Job List tab visible but no filtering shown

15. NO LANGUAGE SUPPORT FOR INSTRUCTIONS IN MULTIPLE LANGUAGES:
    - ACV has English dropdown (good start)
    - But instructions are pre-written and translated
    - Super Dispatch: Automated translation suggested
