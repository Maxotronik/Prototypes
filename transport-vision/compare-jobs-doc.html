01 · Background
The Problem

The ACV Transport Load Board is where carriers browse and accept transport jobs. Evaluating multiple jobs today means opening them one at a time — there's no way to see them side by side while browsing.

The only moment where some comparison is possible today is during vehicle staging, when carriers can see a handful of jobs together. Compare Jobs moves that capability earlier in the flow — into the browsing experience itself — and could eventually replace the staging step as the primary place to evaluate and decide.

02 · Goals
What We're Trying to Achieve
1
Restore the ability for carriers to compare multiple jobs at once without navigating away from the Loadboard or losing their place in the list.
2
Ensure the compare experience works alongside — not against — the modal job detail view and Instant Book.
3
Reduce the cognitive load of job selection so carriers can make faster, more confident booking decisions.
03 · Requirements
Feature Requirements
  • Carriers can select multiple jobs from the load board and compare them side by side without leaving the list.
  • The comparison surfaces all relevant vehicle details — price, route, condition, handling requirements, and location access — so no information is missing at decision time.
  • Carriers can stage or instant-book any compared job directly from the comparison view.
04 · Solution Design
How It Works
Excluded job types

Two job types are excluded from Compare Jobs and do not display a selection checkbox on their cards.

local_offer Has Offer
  • Has Offer vehicles already have an active offer in negotiation. They cannot be staged, only Instant Booked at the original posted price.
  • Including them in the compare modal would introduce an asymmetry — Stage disabled for some columns, available for others — that adds complexity without clear benefit at this stage.
  • Carriers can still Instant Book a Has Offer vehicle directly from the job card on the list.
  • Future consideration: if data shows carriers frequently want to compare Has Offer vehicles before deciding to IB, revisit with a restricted column state (IB only, Stage disabled).
inventory_2 Bundles
  • A bundle is a group of vehicles offered together as a single lot. Vehicle details are not surfaced on the card — they require opening a modal and reviewing each vehicle individually.
  • This structure is incompatible with the Compare modal, which works at the individual vehicle level and expects a single set of field values per column.
Entry point — selection from the job list

Carriers check a checkbox on each job card. A bottom bar appears as soon as 2 jobs are selected, showing the count and a Compare button. The bar persists regardless of which filters are active — selections survive filter changes. Tapping Compare opens the modal without leaving the list. Maximum 5 vehicles.

The bottom bar approach keeps the list alive underneath the comparison. A dedicated comparison page would break the carrier's browsing context — they'd lose filter state and have to rebuild their search to go back.

A table layout enforces field alignment across vehicles — every row is the same data point for all columns, so differences are visible without scanning effort. Cards would put each vehicle's information in isolation.

Smart badges

Inline badges surface notable characteristics per vehicle without requiring the carrier to read and mentally compare values. Each badge is independent — a vehicle can hold multiple at the same time. Badges only appear when there is a meaningful signal (a clear winner, a shared location, an access condition); they are suppressed when all vehicles tie on a dimension.

All badges use the auto_awesome icon alongside the label to distinguish them visually from plain text.

auto_awesome Best Pay Best Pay
  • Marks the job(s) with the highest price per mile.
  • Jobs without a PPM value do not qualify.
  • Shown on all tied jobs when more than one shares the top rate.
auto_awesome Shortest Shortest
  • Marks the job(s) with the fewest route miles.
  • Shown on all tied jobs when more than one shares the lowest distance.
auto_awesome Same Location Same location
  • Appears on pickup address cells when two or more compared jobs share the same zip code.
  • Applied independently to delivery addresses.
  • Signals a potential route stack without the carrier having to read and compare addresses manually.
auto_awesome After Hours After Hours
  • Appears in the instructions cell when a location allows after-hours access, below any hours or notes text.
  • When after-hours access is explicitly not allowed, a red indicator is shown instead.
Actions

Each vehicle column has Stage and Instant Book buttons, fixed at the bottom of the modal and always visible without scrolling. After either action is taken, both buttons for that vehicle are replaced with a status label ("Staged" or "Booked") and a green checkmark — the carrier can act on multiple jobs in sequence without closing the view. A Remove button at the top of each column drops that vehicle from the comparison; if fewer than 2 remain, the modal closes.

Actions live inside the modal because the comparison is the decision point. Requiring the carrier to close, find the card, and then act adds steps at exactly the moment when intent is highest.

Instant Book date selection

Tapping Instant Book on a compare column opens an overlay with two date pickers — Pickup Date and Delivery Date. The available window is fixed by route distance: both pickup and delivery must fall within today → today + max SLA days. The carrier chooses when to pick up and when to deliver within that window — delivery only needs to be on or after the chosen pickup date. Changing the pickup date does not reset the delivery date unless it would fall before the new pickup, in which case delivery is moved forward to match.

Two date picker formats are available via a switcher in the Instant Book modal header — the carrier can toggle between them at any time without losing their selected dates:

  • Calendar — two calendar panes side by side (desktop) or stacked (mobile), each with independent month navigation. Days outside the eligible window are grayed out. Best for carriers who want to plan across a longer horizon.
  • Strip — a horizontally scrollable row of day buttons grouped by month label. Both strips span the full SLA window (today → today + max days); days before the selected pickup are shown disabled in the delivery strip. Best for quick, nearby-date selection.
Instant Book — desktop mode

A mode selector in the compare modal header controls how the Instant Book flow opens on desktop. Two options:

  • Accordion — the active vehicle column expands inline, inserting the date picker directly below the row where the carrier clicked Instant Book. The column width stays fixed — the strip scrolls horizontally inside the cell if dates don't fit. Calendars stack vertically when the column is too narrow to show them side by side. The YMM row stays pinned above.
  • Popover — the date picker opens as a floating panel anchored to the Instant Book button. Positioned below the button by default, flips up if there is not enough space below. Width 360px.

Accordion is the default. The mode selector is persistent within the session — switching modes closes any open IB UI before re-opening it in the new mode.

Layout — desktop

Fixed-width modal. Sticky label column on the left (always visible). Vehicle columns scroll horizontally. Zebra striping on all rows including the label column aids readability across many fields.

The YMM row is pinned by JavaScript on scroll — it tracks the table's vertical position and applies a transform to keep the row visible at the top of the scroll area regardless of the browser's overflow context. This ensures vehicle identity is always visible when scrolling through long field lists.

Layout — mobile

Full-screen overlay. One vehicle visible at a time with horizontal snap scroll. Prev/next navigation arrows. Action buttons fixed to the bottom per vehicle. A compressed side-by-side table at mobile widths would reduce every column to an unreadable width — one vehicle per screen trades horizontal density for legibility.

The YMM row is also JS-pinned on mobile, remaining visible at the top of the screen as the carrier scrolls down through the field rows.

05 · Success Metrics
What We're Measuring
Business success
These metrics require a pre-launch baseline to be meaningful. Measure stage-to-contract and stage-to-cancel rates, and overall Instant Book rate, before shipping.
MetricHypothesis
Stage-to-contract rate
Compare modal vs baseline		 Carriers who compare before staging make more informed decisions and follow through at a higher rate.
Stage-to-cancel rate
Compare modal vs baseline		 Fewer cancellations because carriers have already seen all relevant details — price, handling, access — before committing.
Instant Book rate overall
Pre vs post launch		 Faster decision-making in the compare view increases the share of jobs that skip staging entirely.
Instant Book source
Compare modal vs job details modal		 A meaningful share of Instant Books originates from the compare view, confirming it functions as a decision surface.
Strategic signal — can Compare replace staging?

A longer-term question this feature should help answer: if carriers are making informed booking decisions inside the compare modal, does the staging step become redundant? Track the ratio of direct Instant Books from Compare vs. jobs that go through Stage first. If Compare-driven IB grows consistently, it opens the door to simplifying the booking flow — and to revisiting the exclusion of Has Offer vehicles, which are already IB-only and could fit naturally into a Compare-first model.

Usage & behavior
  • % of sessions that use Compare at least once; % of unique carriers who use the feature; repeat usage rate.
  • Distribution of vehicles per compare session (2, 3, 4, 5) — reveals typical comparison depth.
  • % of compare sessions that end in an action (stage or IB) vs closed without acting.
  • Remove button usage — how often carriers drop a vehicle mid-comparison, and at what point.
  • Time in modal before action — proxy for how much cognitive work the modal is absorbing.
  • Do jobs with a Best Pay or Shortest badge get contracted at a higher rate than jobs without?
  • Does the Same location badge correlate with both jobs in that pair being contracted in the same session?
